QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Indian Business Bureau is a community-focused platform designed to connect people, businesses and legitimate opportunities through a structured ecosystem.

₹101 is the current one-time IBB membership and registration fee. It provides membership processing and applicable community access according to IBB terms.

You register, complete the ₹101 payment, submit your transaction reference and wait for manual verification. After verification, applicable membership/community instructions are provided.

Business requirements can be structured into campaigns. Eligible members can review available campaigns, follow instructions, complete the required work and submit proof or output for review.

Examples include lead generation, market research, local promotion, business outreach, content creation, data verification, customer research and social media support. Availability depends on actual campaigns.

Campaign submissions can be checked against the specific campaign instructions and eligibility requirements before approval.

Where a campaign includes a reward, the applicable amount, eligibility, approval conditions and other terms are defined by that campaign. Participation does not itself guarantee a particular reward.

Businesses can submit their requirements through the business enquiry section. IBB can then evaluate whether the requirement is suitable for a community campaign.

After the UPI payment, the member submits the transaction reference/UTR. The information is manually reviewed before membership processing.

Yes. The rating interface allows members to provide genuine feedback. Published ratings and testimonials should represent real experiences and verified submissions.

Individual campaigns and opportunities may have separate eligibility requirements, instructions, deadlines and terms. Review the applicable campaign information before participating.
