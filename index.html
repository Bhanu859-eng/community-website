Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Indian Business Bureau kya hai?

Indian Business Bureau (IBB) ek community-focused platform hai jo people, businesses aur legitimate opportunities ko connect karne ke liye designed hai. Businesses apni genuine requirements ko campaigns/tasks ke form mein introduce kar sakte hain.

IBB business ke liye kya karta hai?

Business ki requirement ko structured campaign format mein organise kiya ja sakta hai. Campaign ke nature ke according lead generation, research, outreach, promotion, content, data support ya other legitimate tasks ho sakte hain.

Member ko kya karna hota hai?

Eligible member available campaign ko review kar sakta hai, eligibility aur instructions samajh sakta hai, task complete karke required proof/work submit kar sakta hai. Approval ke baad campaign ke defined payout terms apply hote hain.

Task complete karne par payment guaranteed hai?

Kisi bhi task ka payout campaign ke specific terms, eligibility, submission requirements aur approval par depend karta hai. IBB membership ya participation ko guaranteed income ke roop mein present nahi kiya jata.

₹101 kisliye hai?

₹101 current one-time IBB membership/registration fee hai. Ye kisi specific job, task, lead ya guaranteed earning ko purchase karne ki payment nahi hai.

Kya ₹101 dene ke baad earning start ho jayegi?

Nahi. Membership se automatic earning guarantee nahi hoti. Future campaigns ki availability, eligibility aur payout terms alag ho sakti hain.

Payment kaise verify hota hai?

UPI payment complete karne ke baad member UTR/transaction ID submit karta hai. Payment information ko manual verification process ke through check kiya ja sakta hai.

Community access kab milega?

Registration aur payment verification ke baad applicable community access instructions eligible members ko provide kiye ja sakte hain.

Kya IBB guaranteed jobs provide karta hai?

Nahi. IBB membership ko guaranteed employment, job, income ya profit ke promise ke roop mein nahi lena chahiye. Opportunities campaign-specific aur availability-based ho sakti hain.

Kya IBB par fake reviews ya fake earning claims hain?

IBB ko genuine member feedback aur transparent information ke around build kiya jana chahiye. Fake ratings, fabricated testimonials aur guaranteed earning claims publish nahi kiye jane chahiye.

Community se shuruaat karo.

IBB ke early member network ka part baniye. ₹101 current one-time membership fee hai. Join karne se pehle membership terms aur opportunity conditions ko samajhna important hai.
