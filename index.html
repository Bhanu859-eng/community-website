FAQ

Clear answers. No confusion.

What is Indian Business Bureau?

IBB is being built as a community-first platform connecting people, businesses and legitimate opportunities.

What is the ₹101 payment?

It is the proposed one-time membership/verification fee for joining the IBB community. Final membership terms, benefits and refund policy should be published before accepting live payments.

Will every member definitely earn money?

No. Participation in future campaigns or opportunities is not a guaranteed source of income. Eligibility and campaign terms will depend on the specific opportunity.

How will I join the community after payment?

After confirmed payment, the website can provide secure access to the official community onboarding channel. The live payment and access system will be connected after the business setup is finalized.
