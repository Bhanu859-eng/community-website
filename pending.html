Frequently Asked Questions

What is Indian Business Bureau?

Indian Business Bureau is a community-focused platform intended to connect people, businesses and legitimate opportunities.

Why is the membership ₹101?

₹101 is the current one-time membership fee for joining the IBB community.

Does membership guarantee earnings?

No. Membership does not guarantee earnings, employment, profit or a specific financial return. Any paid opportunity must be evaluated according to its own eligibility and terms.

How is my payment verified?

After making the UPI payment, members submit their UTR/transaction ID. The submitted information can then be checked before membership access is provided.

How do I get community access?

After registration and successful payment verification, eligible members receive the applicable community access instructions.

Can I see actual member feedback?

Yes. Genuine member feedback can be published on this page as the community grows. Testimonials should always represent real experiences.
